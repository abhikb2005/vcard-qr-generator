About vCard QR Code Generator

vCard QR Code Generator helps professionals create contact QR codes for business cards, badges, resumes, event materials, and email signatures.

Privacy-first contact sharing

The free browser generator renders static QR codes in the browser so contact data can stay on the user's device. Public developer endpoints return vCard text and product metadata for automation workflows; they do not create accounts or store contacts.
